# About The Saffron Grand, Indore

## Our Story

The Saffron Grand opened in 2015 in Vijay Nagar, Indore's commercial hub, and has grown into one of the city's landmark 5-star properties. What began as a single 40-room business hotel expanded over the years into a 180-room full-service luxury property with multiple restaurants, a spa, and dedicated banquet and conference facilities — now a fixture for both business travelers and destination weddings in Indore.

## Setting

Centrally located in Vijay Nagar, close to major corporate offices, malls, and a short drive from both Indore Junction and the airport. The hotel is positioned as a premium business and leisure destination, not a budget stay.

## Dining

The Saffron Grand runs four distinct dining venues under one roof, each with its own identity — from all-day Indian multi-cuisine dining to a rooftop lounge with city views. Guests can dine at any venue regardless of which room category they're staying in, and in-room dining is available from Saffron Kitchen's menu around the clock.

## Events and Business

The hotel's banquet halls and conference rooms host everything from corporate conferences to weddings, and the business center supports travelers needing meeting space or work facilities during their stay.

## Who We Serve

A mix of corporate travelers, wedding parties, and leisure guests, along with local residents who visit specifically for the restaurants, spa, or pool without staying at the hotel.
